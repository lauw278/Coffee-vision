# LinkedIn launch post — CoffeeVision

I’m sharing an early-stage project I’ve been exploring: **CoffeeVision — an AI-assisted coffee ripeness and defect classification prototype.**

The idea is to explore how computer vision could support the visual assessment of coffee cherries at different ripeness stages, with a related future direction for coffee bean defect assessment.

The current project includes:
- A Jupyter Notebook outlining experiments with CNN, ResNet18, InceptionResNetV2, and YOLO classification workflows.
- A browser-based image upload and AI-assisted analysis prototype.
- A documented plan for validating the dataset, evaluating models, and connecting a secure inference backend.

An important note: substantial portions of the initial code were generated with AI assistance. The notebook has not yet been independently executed and validated, so I am not claiming model accuracy or production readiness. I’m treating this as a learning and engineering project: reviewing generated code, identifying gaps, and working toward a reproducible implementation.

**Next milestone:** validate a four-class coffee cherry ripeness workflow, evaluate it on a held-out test set, and connect a verified model to the application.
